No ticket? No problem. FIFA World Cup 2026 Fan Fests are officially sanctioned free viewing parties hosted across all 16 host cities — and beyond. With giant screens, live entertainment, food vendors, and thousands of fellow fans, Fan Fests are often considered the best way to experience the World Cup atmosphere without ever setting foot in a stadium.

Here's your complete guide to Fan Fest locations, what to expect, and how to make the most of them.

## What Is a FIFA Fan Fest?

FIFA Fan Fests are official free public viewing events set up in major public spaces across host cities. First introduced at the 2006 World Cup in Germany, they've become a signature feature of every tournament — attracting millions of visitors who watch matches on massive LED screens while enjoying live music, food and drink, and the electric energy of thousands of international fans gathered in one place.

The 2026 edition spans three countries, making it the most geographically widespread Fan Fest program in World Cup history.

## USA Fan Fest Locations

### New York / New Jersey
**Location:** Times Square & Central Park, Manhattan

New York's Fan Fest is expected to be the largest in the world, particularly for the Final. Times Square transforms into a sea of football fans from every nation, with giant screens broadcast on the famous LED billboards. Central Park's Great Lawn hosts the main official viewing area with capacity for tens of thousands.

**Best for:** The Final and knockout stage matches — the atmosphere is unmatched anywhere in the world.

**Location:** Grand Park, Downtown LA

## Fan Fest Tips

**Arrive early.** Fan Fests fill up quickly for high-profile matches. For knockout stage games and anything involving the host nations, arrive 2–3 hours before kickoff to secure a good spot.

**Check local schedules.** Each city publishes its own Fan Fest program with opening times, entertainment acts, and special events. Check the official city tourism websites closer to the tournament for confirmed schedules.

**It's free but bring cash.** Entry to Fan Fests is free, but food, drinks, and merchandise inside are paid. Most vendors accept cards but cash is always useful as queues can be long.

**Dress for the weather.** June and July temperatures vary hugely across the host cities — from Miami's humidity to Seattle's cool evenings to Dallas's extreme heat. Check forecasts and plan accordingly.

**Stay safe.** Fan Fests attract massive crowds. Keep personal belongings secure, stay with your group, and follow instructions from event staff and security.
